title: Ursinia - Bible - Reading Plan - Primer
---

The main idea is to read through the whole Bible, in the order below, at
about **four chapters a day**. That finishes the list in roughly nine
months. Some entries are whole books and some are ranges of chapters;
the day count on each one assumes that pace, so you can see what you are
signing up for before you start.

The order is not the order the books are printed in, and it is not
chronological. It is the order that makes the story land on someone who
has never read any of it. You get twelve days of setup - creation, the
fall, the promise to Abraham, the rescue out of Egypt, the law, and then
four hundred years of silence - and then you meet Christ, and you stay
with Him for four months. Everything after that is you going back to
fill in the world He walked into, having already met Him.

Because of that, a few books are split. You read Genesis 1-22 in the
first week and Genesis 23-50 four months later. That is deliberate. The
first half is what you need to understand John; the second half reads
differently once you do.

Alongside the main reading, keep a second, smaller slot every day:

- **Month one**: read the [Proverb](/bible/versions/bsb/text/#proverbs) that
  matches the day of the month. January 1st is Proverbs 1, January
  31st is Proverbs 31.
- **Months two through seven**: read one
  [Psalm](/bible/versions/bsb/text/#psalm) a day, straight through. The
  Psalms are prayers, not a story, and they are worth one at a time
  rather than a hundred and fifty in a row.
- **The rest of the plan**: memorization. Pick a passage or passages -
  the ones from the Psalms that would not leave you are a good place
  to start - and store them in your heart.
